Truth in Love

“We love because He first loved us.” — 1 John 4:19

Who Is Jesus?

Jesus is not just a historical figure. He is the Son of God, the Savior, the King, and the only way to eternal life. He came to earth to live a perfect life and die for our sins; He rose again to conquer death; and He is coming back, just as He promised.

  • He lived a perfect, sinless life.
  • He died and rose again to save us.
  • He will return and make all things new.
“I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.” — John 14:6

What Jesus Accomplished by Dying on the Cross

  • Took our sin and guilt: Jesus carried the weight of our sins, past, present, future.
  • Satisfied justice: He endured the penalty we deserved.
  • Displayed God’s love: The cross is the clearest picture of love.
  • Defeated death: His resurrection broke sin’s power.
  • Opened the way to God: Our relationship with the Father is restored by grace.
  • Gave new identity: In Christ, we are made new.
  • Showed compassion: Jesus healed and set people free.
“For this purpose the Son of God was revealed: to destroy the works of the devil.” — 1 John 3:8

Repentance — Turning Back to God

What is repentance? Turning away from sin and turning toward God. It’s a heart change that leads to new life.

Why repent? Everyone sins, and sin separates us from God. “All have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.” — Romans 3:23

  1. Admit your need for God.
  2. Confess your sins honestly.
  3. Decide to turn and follow Jesus.
  4. Believe He died and rose for you.
  5. Walk in new life daily.

“Repent and be baptized…” — Acts 2:38

Prayer — Speaking with the Father

Prayer is how we connect with God—honest, personal, and powerful.

  • In all circumstances: James 5:13
  • Healing and help: James 5:14
  • Powerful and effective: James 5:16
  • Confess and pray for one another: James 5:16
  • Prayer changes things: James 5:179–18

The God We Serve — The Holy Trinity

We worship one God in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

  • The Father creates and sustains.
  • The Son (Jesus) saves and reigns.
  • The Holy Spirit comforts, guides, and empowers.
“Baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit.” — Matthew 28:19

Living in Love

Jesus commands us to love like He loves—selflessly and boldly. Love is the evidence of our faith. “Let all that you do be done in love.” — 1 Corinthians 16:14

Why We Share the Gospel

We share because we’ve been loved and rescued, and we want others to know hope and freedom in Jesus. “Go and make disciples of all nations.” — Matthew 28:19

Ready for His Return

Jesus promised He is coming back. Until then, we live in His love and truth, sharing hope with everyone.
